    Every command is a card

    Output stays with the command that printed it, with its exit code and how long it took. A failed run turns red, and one click asks Claude why.

    Copy the command or the output, run it again, or explain the failure.

    Programs that ask get a field

    When a script stops to ask a yes or no, or a password, the composer turns into a question field with the answers it expects. Passwords stay hidden as you type.

    Esc hands the keys back to the terminal whenever you want them.

    One palette, and a terminal from anywhere

    ⌘K finds every menu command, slash command, tab, theme, snippet and recent folder, with the ones you use most first. Themes preview as you arrow past them, and > searches your history.

    ⌃` slides a Quick Terminal down over whatever app you are in, and it keeps its session between visits. Press it again, or Esc on an empty prompt, to put it away.

    It knows what project you're in

    Node, Swift, Rust, Go, Python, Make, just or Docker Compose: a chip in the composer opens the project's tasks, with its branch and changes beside them. ⏎ puts a task in the composer, ⌘⏎ runs it.

    Pull, push, switch branch and stash are one click away, and they only fill in the command. A quiet chip warns when your node or python doesn't match .nvmrc or .python-version.

    Long output, under control

    Collapse every card with ⌥⌘←, search with ⌘F and step with ⌘G. Quit and come back: Cue reopens your windows, tabs and splits where you left them, with the last lines of each pane above a "Restored from" line.

    Share a command as an image

    Any card becomes a PNG: the command, its status, the folder and the colored output, in your theme's window on a gradient, the theme or nothing at all. Secrets are redacted before you copy it.

    Those are straight out of Cue, untouched.

    Your own Claude, one keystroke away

    Explain the last error with ⌥⌘E, fix the last command with ⌥⌘F, or describe what you want with ⌘I and get a command back.

    • Your key or your CLIUse an Anthropic API key, kept in the macOS Keychain, or the claude CLI you already sign in to.
    • Secrets stay outTokens, passwords and keys in a command or its output are redacted before anything is sent.
    • Nothing in betweenRequests go from your Mac straight to Anthropic, or through your local claude. No account, no server of ours.

    Keys you'll reach for

    ⌃R history, ⇧⌘P snippets, ⌘T a new tab, ⌘D a split, ⌘B folds the sidebar, ⌘K opens the palette.

    Type on your keyboard. The keys light up with yours.
